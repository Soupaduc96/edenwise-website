About EdenWise

Cultivating wisdom. Restoring hope.

A ministry that guides young people, engaged couples and married couples to understand and live by God’s design for marriage and family.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

The mission of EdenWise is to guide young people, engaged couples and married couples to understand and live according to God’s design for marriage.

Through biblical teaching, counseling, prayer, conferences and practical resources, we equip individuals and families to build healthy relationships, fulfil their God-given destinies and live marriage under Kingdom wisdom. We serve everyone who seeks guidance, with a special focus on the Haitian community.

Mission

To guide young people, engaged and married couples in living by God’s design for marriage through biblical teaching, counseling and prayer.

Vision

To see individuals, couples and families walking in God’s design for marriage, living as Kingdom-minded people in healthy, strong, covenant-centered relationships.

Values

Rooted in biblical truth, covenant marriage, wisdom-centered living, love with responsibility, healing, holistic growth and Kingdom impact.

    The Blueprint of Love

    Read

    “This is my commandment, That ye love one another, as I have loved you.”

    John 15:12 (KJV)

    Jesus does not leave love undefined. He gives it a measure: as I have loved you. That measure moves love out of feelings alone and into covenant. It becomes patient, costly, forgiving and faithful.

    In counseling we often see couples who love each other sincerely but have no shared blueprint. Each partner builds from their own family’s plans, their own wounds and their own expectations. Christ offers a better blueprint. It is one we learn together and practise daily. It turns small, repeated choices into a house that stands.

    Reflect
    1. Where have I measured love by how I feel instead of how Christ loves?
    2. What one small act of costly love can I offer today?
    3. Is there a hurt I need to bring to God before I can love freely?
    Begin
    Practise · Breath Prayer

    Breathe in: “Lord Jesus, You have loved me.”
    Breathe out: “Teach me to love like You.”

    Questions

    Frequently asked.

    01Is counseling confidential?

    Yes. Your intake and sessions are shared only with your assigned counselor. The only exceptions are situations where someone’s safety is at risk, and we explain these limits clearly at your first session.

    02Do I need to be a Christian to receive care?

    No. Our care is openly Christ-centered, and everyone seeking guidance is welcome. We never pressure you. We invite you.

    03Are sessions online or in person?

    Both. You can meet by secure video from anywhere, or in person in Fort-Liberté, Haïti.

    04What happens if I cancel my membership?

    You keep access until the end of your current billing period. Your account then moves to the free Pilgrim level, and your progress is preserved.

    05Do you offer multi-user family plans?

    Yes. The Covenant Family tier is built for households and gives up to four family members their own profile and journey.
